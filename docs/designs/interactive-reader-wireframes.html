Storykins · Interactive Reader · Wireframe

Three ways to steer a story, beat by beat

Same story, same controls, three different layouts to compare. In interactive mode a story unfolds one beat at a time: press ▶ Continue to let the storyteller decide, pick one of 3 directions (or write your own) to steer, and watch the arc-budget bar fill — then snap to “The End” when the tale wraps up naturally. Pick the direction that feels right and we lock it into the plan.

Actually try it: tap ▶, pick a choice, or type your own — in all three.
Direction A · ✓ Chosen for v1

Scroll & Reveal

The story grows as one continuous storybook page. A persistent bar at the bottom holds ▶ and choices.

Best for calm, immersive reading · trade-off controls compete with a long scroll.

Direction B

Beat Cards

One beat per card, front and centre. Tap to advance like a visual novel; segmented progress up top.

Best for focus & a game-like feel · trade-off less “curl-up-and-read.”

Direction C

Immersive Ambient

Big text over a soft illustration wash. Controls hide behind a floating “What next?” sheet; progress as quiet dots.

Best for bedtime & wonder · trade-off hidden controls = one extra tap.

Reading this wireframe: the story (Pip and the Silver Bell) and its choices are identical across all three on purpose — you’re judging layout, not writing. Fonts (Fredoka/Nunito) and the cover art are approximated here; the shipped app uses the real ones. Everything is clickable: advance to the end in each to feel the pacing and the progress-bar snap. Toggle light/dark — both are designed.
