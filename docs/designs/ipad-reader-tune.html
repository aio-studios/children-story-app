What changes (single-column tune)

  • Card width at tablet breakpoints: 428 → ~600 px so it stops floating in empty canvas.
  • Hero image scales with the card — bigger, more immersive cover.
  • Body text 18 → 19 px, title up to ~32 px, more generous padding & gaps for couch reading.
  • Line length stays capped at 34 em — the existing guard means wider card ≠ unreadably long lines.
  • Buttons sit at natural width instead of stretching the full wide card.
  • Applies to both the classic and interactive readers (they share the same tokens). Phone layout is untouched.
