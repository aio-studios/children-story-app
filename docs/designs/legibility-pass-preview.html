What's changing & why

  • Nothing below ~11px survives. Genre labels go 9.6px → 12.5px, stepper labels 10px → 12.5px, eyebrows/tags ~10px → 11px.
  • Body text lands at 16px. Mascot line, pills, fields, card names all hit a real base size instead of 13.6–14px.
  • Titles get authority. Section titles 15→18px, hero title 17→21px, step heading 18→24px.
  • Tap targets clear ~44px. Pills, nav buttons, and fields grow padding to meet Apple's minimum.
  • One shared scale, not per-component bumps — new --sk-fs-* tokens, so it can't drift small again.
