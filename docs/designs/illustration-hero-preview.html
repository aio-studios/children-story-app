The Fox Who Forgot the Moon

Little Pip the fox had a very important job. Every night, she tucked the moon into its blanket of clouds so the whole forest could sleep.

But one sleepy evening, Pip yawned so wide that she forgot exactly where she'd left it. "Oh no," she whispered. "A forest can't sleep without its moon."

So Pip asked the owl, the hedgehog, and even the grumpy old toad — and one by one, each friend helped her remember a little more of the way.

The "Loaded" image is a hand-drawn SVG stand-in for what Gemini 2.5 Flash Image ("Nano Banana") would generate from the story's character sheet — used only to show placement, framing, and the rounded 4:3 slot. Real output will be a full raster illustration.
