Prince Oren and the Magic Door

Prince Oren had always been different. While other princes wanted to fight dragons, Oren wanted to be their friend.

One day, Oren found an old oak door in the forest. It was hidden behind tall trees. The light around it bent and shimmered like water.

"Hello, door," Oren said kindly. "What's beyond you?"

The door creaked open. Beyond it was a garden Oren had never seen before, full of flowers that hummed soft little tunes when the wind passed through them.

A small green dragon poked its head out from behind a hedge. It looked more curious than fierce, tilting its head at the prince the way a puppy might.

"You're not here to fight me?" the dragon asked, surprised.

"Why would I?" said Oren. "You look like you'd rather show me around."

The dragon's eyes lit up. It had lived in this garden for a hundred years and no one had ever asked to see it before.
