The chosen setup direction — B "Immersive deck" — adapted to phone portrait, phone landscape, and iPad. Same content and logic; the layout reshapes to the space. Switch form factor and theme; tap cards, arrows, dots, and the Back chip. This closes the last design gap before /create-plan.
